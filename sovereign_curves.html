Source: Trading Economics, over-the-counter interbank yield quotes. On opening and on every refresh, the page reads each market's Trading Economics page through a public relay (r.jina.ai, then allorigins, then codetabs, as in the Poste de marché file) and redraws with the latest yields, changes and policy rates. A green dot on a card means live data, a red dot means the fetch failed and the built-in snapshot of 29 Sep to 1 Oct 2026 is shown. Policy rates are refreshed too; China's PBoC 7-day reverse repo and Singapore's SORA are read from their own Trading Economics pages. Curves for one day, one month and one year ago are rebuilt from the changes published for each maturity. YTD is measured on the 10-year only, against the 2025 year-end close implied by Trading Economics' YTD column (about ±1 bp of rounding); the source does not publish year-end levels for other maturities. Mexico, Chile and Colombia are only published at the 10-year point. This is a snapshot taken at publication time, not a live feed.
